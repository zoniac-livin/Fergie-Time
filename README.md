# ⏱️ Fergie Time

> *"Can you turn €250M into a multi-billion euro football empire before the final whistle?"*

**Fergie Time** is a fast-paced football portfolio and market management game inspired by *The Front Three* (GOAL). 

Managers start with a strict **€250M budget** to assemble a 7-a-side squad via blind stat profiles. Navigate a dynamic **10-year market cycle** governed by historical valuation curves: spot undervalued breakout talent, cash out on superstars at their absolute peak, dump depreciating assets, and maximize total squad valuation before the clock runs out.

---

## 🎯 Gameplay Mechanics

- **Blind Scouting Engine:** Player identities remain hidden during the draft. Evaluate targets strictly on age, radar percentiles, key per-90 metrics, and initial transfer price tags.
- **The €250M Cap:** Allocate capital across 7 designated squad positions (1 GK, 2 DEF, 2 MID, 2 ATT) without breaching budget constraints.
- **10-Year Valuation Engine:** Advance through annual market updates driven by real-world historical player trajectory curves—factoring in prime age plateaus, sudden injury drops, and breakout seasons.
- **Transfer Deadlines:** Execute hold/sell decisions at designated transfer windows. Reinvest realized liquid capital into emerging prospects or compound cash reserves.
- **The Whistle (Year 10):** Receive an executive performance audit evaluating Total Portfolio Value, Net ROI against the market benchmark, and managerial tier rating.
